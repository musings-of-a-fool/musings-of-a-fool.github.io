The Ransom of Jesus
"Just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve and to give his life as a ransom for many" Matthew 20:28 Historically, even up to today, the sacrifice of Jesus of Nazareth has been flattened, boxed in, and subverted. We talk of the sacrifice as if the crucifixion itself were it, rather than it being the culmination and final note of it all. So what was the sacrifice? Perhaps not his death, but his life… of being misunderstood, hated, and accused. The sacrifice was his decision to veil the immediacy of absolute unity for the sake of walking on two legs, having a body, and a mouth to speak the hated truth. His death? His death was a ransom. Ransoms are not paid to the rightful owner. They are paid to the thief so that what is rightfully theirs may be returned back to them. The cost was blood. Who is paying whom? God loves us. And he desires mercy, not sacrifice. Acknowledgment, not burnt offerings. So... what happens when we confuse the captor and creator? Perhaps the truth gets nailed to a cross and bleeds until we finally confront ourselves, paying attention to who killed whom, who paid whom, and why. The crucifixion did not free us through atonement. It frees us by revealing the identity of the captor. Jesus lived to teach the true way to the Father and he had to die so we could know who killed him... The cross was never God demanding blood.. it was God revealing who does.
